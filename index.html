    Privacy Policy
    Info

    Ray Isaac Tracker helps you track progress in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance by showing you which achievements you haven't unlocked yet.

    The Percentage (%) column shows the percentage of players that have unlocked that achievement.

    The Character and Boss columns show the character or boss required to unlock that achievement.

    This app is inspired by Rebirth Achievement Helper and uses data from the Rebirth Wiki.

    Source code is publicly available on GitHub.

    Upload Save
    This tool reads your local persistentgamedata#.dat save file for The Binding of Isaac: Repentance. The file never leaves your browser — it's parsed entirely on your device.
    On Windows, save files are usually found in %USERPROFILE%\Documents\My Games\Binding of Isaac Repentance+ or in Steam's userdata folder (Steam\userdata\<your id>\250900\remote\). The number in the filename (1, 2, or 3) matches the save slot you use in-game.
    Privacy Policy

    This website uses your browser's local storage functionality to keep track of your Binding of Isaac save progress. This is automatically stored on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

    Your uploaded save file itself is never sent anywhere — it's read and parsed entirely in your browser. To remove your progress data from your browser, click the "Remove Progress" button in the "Upload Save" tool. Additionally, you can clear your browser's local storage to remove all data stored by this website.
